Questions worth asking.

A few things you may want to know before keeping your first second.

Memakho is a private space for keeping the small moments that usually disappear. Capture a little from your day, and let those moments quietly become a record of your life.

Memakho is designed around private, local-first memory keeping. Your memories should remain under your control rather than becoming part of a public social feed.

Memakho is built around the idea that your memories are personal. The product is designed to avoid turning your moments into a social feed or something to perform for others.

Memakho is designed to feel simple and personal, without unnecessary friction.

Your memories should remain yours to manage. The experience is designed around keeping, revisiting, and removing moments on your terms.

Memakho is being created for mobile, with the experience designed around making it easy to keep a little moment wherever you are.

Memakho gives you access to a unique way of capturing moments and preserving them for years to come.
